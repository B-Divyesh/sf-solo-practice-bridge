Privacy, in plain terms

Solo Practice Bridge works without an account. Your plans and reflections stay in IndexedDB in this browser.

What stays on your device

Normal use sends no practice records to us. There is no analytics, advertising, microphone access, audio grading, or cloud sync.

Export JSON or CSV when you want a copy. Clearing site data, private browsing, or losing a device can erase local records.

License verification

If you restore an eligible Studio license, its token stays in localStorage. The app sends that token to Sociobot billing for verification.

The free workbook never needs this request. Checkout remains unavailable until billing registration is complete.

Your choices

Export records from Your data. Clear this site’s storage to delete local records. Remove a saved license in the unlock panel.

We cannot recover records that stay only in your browser.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in.
