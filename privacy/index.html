Privacy, in plain terms.

Solo Practice Bridge is designed to work without an account. Your repertoire plans, obstacles, drills, success cues, and session reflections are stored in IndexedDB in your browser. They are not transmitted to us.

What stays on your device

All practice records stay in local browser storage unless you explicitly export a JSON or CSV file. Removing site data, using private browsing, or losing the device may erase them, so exports are the available backup mechanism. There is no analytics, advertising, microphone access, audio grading, or cross-device sync.

License verification

If you buy or restore the optional Studio unlock, the license token is stored in localStorage and sent to Sociobot’s billing API to verify that it is valid for this product. The API returns license status and may process normal server security logs such as IP address and request time. Sociobot/Dodo is the merchant of record for checkout. The free experience never requires this request.

Your choices

Export your records from “Your data.” Clear this site’s storage in your browser to delete local records. Remove a license in the unlock panel. Because practice data does not reach our servers, we cannot recover it or identify it for you.

Contact

For product privacy questions, contact privacy@sociobot.in. This notice may change if storage or paid features change; the effective date above will be updated.
